Customer Segmentation & Revenue Performance Analytics

Business Insights | Growth Analytics | Tableau | Customer Segmentation | Commercial Analytics

Project Overview

Developed Tableau-based customer segmentation and revenue analyses to identify business performance trends, understand revenue drivers, and support data-driven commercial and marketing decisions.

Analyzed historical revenue across customer demographics, acquisition channels, geographic locations, and product categories, translating analytical findings into recommendations for customer targeting, B2B engagement, and growth opportunities.

Analysis Period: August 2020 – January 2021

Business Challenge

The business required greater visibility into which customer segments, acquisition channels, geographic locations, and product categories contributed to revenue performance.

The objective was to provide Marketing with actionable insights to support customer acquisition, marketing planning, and commercial decision-making.

Analytical Approach

Developed three Tableau revenue analyses:

1. Customer Segmentation & Acquisition Channel Analysis

Evaluated revenue contributions by customer age and business source.

Identified major revenue-generating customer segments and channels.

Generated insights to support more focused customer acquisition strategies.

2. Geographic Revenue Performance

Analyzed revenue distribution by customer age group and business location.

Identified geographic differences in customer revenue patterns.

Provided insights to support geographically targeted marketing decisions.

3. Product Revenue Performance

Evaluated revenue across customer age groups and product categories.

Identified differences in product-category revenue contributions.

Highlighted opportunities for more informed customer and product targeting.

Key Business Insights

Identified corporate customers as a significant revenue-generating segment.

Observed variations in revenue across customer demographics, business sources, geographic locations, and product categories.

Provided segmented revenue intelligence to support marketing discussions and planning.

Commercial & Marketing Recommendations

B2B Customer Acquisition: Recommended strengthening LinkedIn engagement to reach corporate decision-makers and support business development.

Digital Marketing: Proposed regular professional content and educational videos to improve brand awareness and customer engagement.

Go-to-Market Support: Suggested product-focused educational content to build awareness of an upcoming car-sharing offering and communicate its value proposition.

Customer Growth: Recommended nurturing corporate customer relationships given their contribution to existing revenue.

Business Value

Connected Tableau revenue analysis with actionable recommendations to support:

Customer segmentation and targeting

Revenue and channel performance analysis

Marketing decision-making

B2B customer acquisition

Product and geographic performance insights

Commercial growth opportunity identification

Findings and recommendations were shared with Marketing for consideration. Implementation results and incremental revenue impact were not measured as part of this analysis.
