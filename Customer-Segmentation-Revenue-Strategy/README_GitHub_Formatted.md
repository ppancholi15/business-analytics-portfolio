# Customer Segmentation & Revenue Performance Analytics

**Business Insights | Growth Analytics | Tableau | Revenue Intelligence | Commercial Analytics**

## Project overview

Developed three complementary Tableau revenue analyses to understand **which customer segments generated revenue, where revenue was concentrated, and how performance differed by product category**. Shared the findings with the Marketing team, along with recommendations for B2B engagement, brand visibility, and product awareness.

**Historical analysis period:** August 1, 2020 – January 5, 2021  
**My contribution:** Revenue analysis, dashboard development, insight communication, and marketing recommendations.  
**Public visuals:** Recreated illustrative heatmaps using synthetic data; **not** the original employer dashboards or values.

## Business challenge

Marketing needed an integrated view of revenue across customer demographics, acquisition sources, locations, and product classes. Analyzing these dimensions together could help inform customer targeting, marketing priorities, and commercial decisions.

## 1. Customer segmentation and acquisition-channel performance

![Illustrative heatmap: revenue by customer age and business source](images/age-business-source.png)

*Illustrative heatmap based on the structure of my original Tableau analysis. All plotted values are synthetic.*

**What I analyzed:** Revenue by customer age group and source of business, including corporate and other customer-acquisition channels.

**Business insight:** The historical analysis highlighted **corporate customers as an important revenue-generating segment**. Comparing source-of-business patterns by age helped provide context for marketing discussions about customer targeting and channel emphasis.

**Business application:** Use segment and channel performance to inform B2B prospecting priorities, customer engagement, and marketing resource allocation. Revenue contribution alone does not establish channel profitability or marketing return on investment.

## 2. Geographic revenue performance

![Illustrative heatmap: revenue by customer age and location](images/age-location.png)

*Illustrative geographic heatmap. Generic location labels and synthetic values replace historical branch information.*

**What I analyzed:** Revenue generated by customer age group across operating locations.

**Business insight:** The analysis provided a way to compare differences in revenue contribution among geographic locations and demographic groups, revealing where performance was concentrated.

**Business application:** Inform localized marketing conversations, assess where further investigation may be valuable, and support more targeted customer outreach. Differences in revenue should be evaluated alongside branch size, demand, and other operational context before reallocating resources.

## 3. Product-class revenue performance

![Illustrative heatmap: revenue by customer age and product class](images/age-product-class.png)

*Illustrative product-category heatmap. Product labels and plotted values are synthetic.*

**What I analyzed:** Revenue by customer age group and product/vehicle class.

**Business insight:** Comparing product classes across demographic segments provided visibility into product revenue concentration and differences in customer purchasing patterns.

**Business application:** Support product-level marketing discussions, identify segments worth examining more closely, and provide inputs for promotional planning. Revenue comparisons alone do not establish customer preference, margin, or causation.

## Recommendations shared with Marketing

### Strengthen B2B acquisition and engagement

Because corporate customers were a significant revenue source, I recommended a stronger LinkedIn presence to support branch-led business development and improve credibility with prospective corporate decision-makers.

### Improve digital brand visibility

Recommended consistent professional updates, relevant hiring announcements, and blog/video content to communicate the company's positioning and reinforce brand awareness.

### Support upcoming product go-to-market awareness

Suggested educational content for a forthcoming car-sharing offering, including **how car sharing works, customer benefits, reasons to choose the business, and service-related information**. These were proposed marketing initiatives, not confirmed launches or measured campaign results.

## Deliverables and business relevance

- Three revenue analysis views connecting demographics with **acquisition sources, locations, and product classes**.
- Analysis shared with Marketing to support planning discussions.
- Practical recommendations spanning **B2B customer engagement, digital marketing, and product awareness**.
- Transferable methods for **Business Insights, Growth Analytics, BI/Reporting, GTM Analytics, Revenue Operations, and Commercial Analytics** roles.
